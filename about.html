About WGIH

A Global Platform for Space, Geospatial and Business Cooperation

Connecting countries, industries and people for a smarter, more sustainable world.

Learn More

Our Mission & Vision

To build a global bridge for geospatial technology, business and innovation, empowering a connected and prosperous world.

WGIH brings space data, practical geospatial solutions and international cooperation into one home — so governments, universities, manufacturers and communities can work from the same map.

Purpose

WGIH is an international platform for geospatial information, space applications, industrial cooperation and skills. This page is a visual profile for the public website prototype.

Approach

The platform connects country offices, solution teams, manufacturers, training programs and partners without acting as an ordinary middleman between buyers and factories.
